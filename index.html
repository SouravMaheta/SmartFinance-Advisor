Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free advisory tool.

Is this tool really 100% free?

Yes. You never pay a single cent. We are supported by partner organizations and referral links when you choose to open a free high-yield savings account or use free financial apps.

Do I need to link my bank account?

No! We will never ask for your bank password, social security number, or account numbers. You simply estimate your round numbers, and the entire simulation runs privately in your browser.

What is the 50/30/20 Rule?

It is the gold standard of personal budgeting: 50% of take-home pay covers essential needs (rent, groceries, utilities), 30% covers lifestyle wants, and 20% is funneled directly into debt elimination and investing.

Debt Snowball vs. Avalanche: Which is better?

The Avalanche method pays off the highest-interest debt first, mathematically saving you the most money. The Snowball method pays the smallest balance first for psychological momentum. Our engine simulates both so you can choose.
